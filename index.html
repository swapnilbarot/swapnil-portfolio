Architecture & strategy

The campaign was built around one principle: precision over reach.

  • 01 Branded-only keyword targeting — focused traffic acquisition on users already searching for the product by name, maximizing relevance from the first impression.
  • 02 Category keyword exclusion — avoided lower-intent, earlier-stage research traffic that would dilute overall query quality.
  • 03 Exact + Phrase Match selection — kept triggered queries closely aligned with actual branded terms rather than loosely related searches.
  • 04 Broad Match exclusion — protected intent purity by preventing algorithmic expansion into unrelated or interpretive queries.

Optimization & management

  • Search Terms Report review to confirm ongoing alignment with branded search intent
  • Continuous negative keyword refinement based on Search Terms Report findings
  • RSA asset optimization to improve message relevance and maintain alignment
  • Auction Insights monitoring to track competitive positioning and impression share trends
